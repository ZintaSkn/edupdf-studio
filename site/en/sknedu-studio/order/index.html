Good to know

        
    
    
  • A licence is tied to the e-mail address of the SknEDU-studio account.
    • 
    
  • It is active on one computer at a time.
    • 
    
  • Transferable: “Deactivate from this PC” in Help › Licence and edition, then activate the same key on the other computer; it keeps its remaining days.
    • 
    
  • Stackable: up to two licences can be active together on one computer, and their days add up.
    • 
    
  • Activation and deactivation need Internet; after that, an automatic check at least every 14 days.
    • 
    
  • When it expires, Premium features stop; the free version remains available.
