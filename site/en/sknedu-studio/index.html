Frequently asked questions

Do I need an Internet connection?

No. SknEDU-studio works offline. Internet is only needed to download the software and its updates, to activate a licence, and for the online AI assistant (the local AI works without Internet).

What happens after the 30-day trial?

The software switches to the free version, with no time limit: read, annotate, organise, fill in, sign, print. Premium features require a Teacher or School licence. The trial is tied to the computer and can only be used once per machine.

How do I pay?

By Wave or MTN MoMo to +225 05 06 84 81 64 (ZintaServices), then send the request and proof of payment by WhatsApp or e-mail. You receive your licence key in return.

Can I move to another computer?

Yes. Deactivate the licence on the old PC (Help › Licence and edition), then activate the same key on the new one: it keeps its remaining days.

Are my documents sent anywhere?

No. Your documents stay on your computer. Nothing is sent without you asking (see the Privacy page).

Windows refuses to install the software?

On some Windows 11 PCs, Smart App Control blocks the installer, which is not signed yet. Download the no-install version (.zip), extract it and run SknEDU-studio.exe: it works and updates normally.
